Protein is the only macro that truly matters right now

For a beginner, hitting your protein target every day is the single most impactful nutrition habit you can build. Protein is what your body uses to repair and build muscle after training. Without enough of it, the training stimulus is wasted — you worked hard in the gym but gave your body nothing to build with.

Hit this number every day. Including rest days. Especially rest days — that's when muscle is actually being built.

What to ignore for now

✕

Don't track carbs or fat obsessively. At your stage, hitting protein and eating mostly whole foods is enough. Micro-optimizing macros is a distraction from what actually matters: consistency and training.

✕

Don't count calories at every meal. Use these targets as a rough guide. Get a feel for portion sizes over a week or two. You don't need a food scale — yet.

✕

Don't cut out entire food groups. No carbs, no fat, no this, no that — these rules make nutrition harder than it is. Eat real food, hit protein, train hard.

✕

Don't buy supplements yet. The only one worth considering is whey protein if you genuinely struggle to hit your protein target from food. Everything else is marketing.
